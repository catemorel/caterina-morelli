Caterina Morelli

I am a Post-doc at the University of Valle d’Aosta, working remotely on energy and tourism in fragile mountain areas. I recently earned my Ph.D. in Economics, Statistics and Data Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca.

My research combines spatial and spatiotemporal methods to study territorial heterogeneity, greenhouse gas emissions, corporate sustainability, energy use and tourism flows. I am always happy to connect around new research collaborations, visiting opportunities and international academic experiences.

About me

I was born in 1996 in Macerata, a small town in the Marche region of central Italy, nestled between the Adriatic Sea and the Apennine Mountains. Growing up in a large family taught me the importance of curiosity, kindness and sharing ideas, while encouraging me to become independent and explore the world.

My academic journey has taken me across Italy and beyond. I earned my Bachelor's degree in Economics in Ancona, including an unforgettable Erasmus experience in Castellón de la Plana, before moving to Rimini for a Master's degree in Statistical, Financial and Actuarial Sciences. These years brought new friendships, inspiring flatmates, plenty of travelling and, of course, a great deal of studying.

After graduation, I worked briefly as a consultant at Deloitte, where I learned to manage pressure and approach complex problems. Although it was a valuable experience, I soon realised that I was more interested in asking new questions than in simply answering existing ones, which led me to pursue a Ph.D.

At the University of Milano-Bicocca, I completed a Ph.D. in Economics, Statistics and Data Science, strengthening my interdisciplinary background through environmental and sustainability applications. Travelling also shaped my research interests: experiencing remarkable natural landscapes, while witnessing the environmental challenges faced by many places, increased my interest in using data and quantitative methods to support more sustainable policies.

My Ph.D. years also brought valuable collaborations, lasting friendships and people with whom I continue to share my passion for research and travelling. After completing my Ph.D. in 2026, I joined the University of Valle d'Aosta as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow, where I currently work on the SMARTDECO project, investigating energy efficiency and tourism in fragile mountain areas.

Research Projects


Below are the research projects in which I have been involved and to which I have contributed.

SMARTDECO

Position
Research Fellow
Period
December 2025 – June 2027
Institution
University of Valle d'Aosta
Description

Energy and Tourism in Fragile Mountain Areas: Strategies and Methodologies for Decoupling is a research project funded under the “R&D Aggregations – Ecological Transition” call of the ERDF Valle d'Aosta 2021–2027 programme. My contribution focuses on the empirical analysis of the environmental and energy impacts of tourism in Valle d'Aosta, considering the heterogeneous effects of different tourism segments and the role of accommodation and bed concentration in energy efficiency.
